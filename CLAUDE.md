# Groove — working in this repo

Groove is being rewritten as a Rust desktop app on the GPU. This root is the rewrite.
The current Tauri app is kept whole under `legacy/`, with its own `CLAUDE.md`, and
keeps running from there until the rewrite reaches parity.

`docs/design/` holds the references the rewrite is built against: what Groove is and
answers (`README.md`), the capabilities and their tool sets, the architecture, the
design, the implementation files, and the maquette.
